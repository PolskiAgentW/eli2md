# Published consolidated texts vs the previous consolidated text in HTML (dziennik-ustaw-md, 2026-10-04)

Measurement only; the converter and the data are not changed. Scripts: `eval/tj_survey/` (they import
`legal_cite.core` from [apiotrowski-afk/legal-cite-pl](https://github.com/apiotrowski-afk/legal-cite-pl) at
a855ad1, Apache-2.0, for the article units of the ELI HTML; paths to the data and the caches are set at the top of
each script). Numbers: `eval/tj_survey/summary_2026-10-04.json`, `eval/tj_survey/triage_2026-10-04.json`.

To check one act (e.g. your own eli2md run of a consolidated text): `eval/tj_survey/tj_check.py` (added
2026-10-04) does the same comparison with paths as arguments: Markdown vs an earlier consolidated text in HTML, vs
another Markdown of the same act, or only vs the PDF; usage and measured results in its docstring.

## Question and method

Since 2025 the ELI API gives consolidated texts (teksty jednolite) of Dziennik Ustaw only as PDF, so
[dziennik-ustaw-md](https://github.com/PolskiAgentW/dziennik-ustaw-md) has them as Markdown converted from the PDF.
How close is that text to the official one?

For every statute (ustawa) with a consolidated text in the dataset (404), the newest one in the dataset was
compared article by article with the newest *earlier* consolidated text of the same statute that the API has in HTML
(article units from the HTML markers, `data-id="arti_…"`; the first unit of a number, i.e. the version in force).
An article that no amendment touched in between must have the same text. Comparison after removing all whitespace,
footnote markers and with superscripts as digits.

Articles that differ were then compared with the text layer of the same PDF (`pdftotext`): from the PDF side,
page headers, footnote texts (taken from the Markdown's footnotes), structure headings after the article and
`N)` markers (also removed from the Markdown side: a footnote number and a point number look the same in
pdftotext) are dropped. What remains is "to review". On the three codes checked by hand first (KPC 2026/468,
KC 2026/795, KP 2026/1245) this step flags exactly the 3 articles with defects found by hand and nothing else.

Converter of the compared files: eli2md 0.6.7 (372), 0.6.22 (5), 0.6.13 (4).

## Results

| | |
|---|---:|
| statutes with a consolidated text in the dataset | 404 |
| no earlier consolidated text in HTML (not compared) | 23 |
| compared | 381 |
| articles in both texts | 50 823 |
| identical | 43 531 (85.7%) |
| different | 7 292 |
| — same as the PDF exactly | 1 932 |
| — same as the PDF without footnotes / page headers | 5 074 |
| — to review | 286 |
| articles only in the newer text (not checked) | 1 182 |
| articles only in the older text | 444 |

So 7 006 of the 7 292 differences are amendments made after the older consolidated text, not conversion errors.

The 286 articles to review, by the pattern of the difference (`tj_triage.py`, first matching class):

| class | articles | statutes | what it is |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| layout | 92 | 61 | every difference ≤ 3 characters: order of `a)` labels, hyphenation in the PDF text layer, footnote numbers glued to numbers; in the cases looked at, the Markdown was right |
| range line | 61 | 57 | `Art. 22–28. (pominięte)` / `(uchylone)` is not a heading, so it ends the previous article (DU/2025/1584 art. 13) |
| footnote in the text | 47 | 36 | a footnote labelled `I)` / `a)` at the end of the act (`Niniejsza ustawa wdraża…`, `Zmiany wymienionego rozporządzenia…`) is written at the end of the last article instead of as a footnote (DU/2025/1131 art. 279); counted also where the PDF has it and the Markdown does not |
| formula | 35 | 15 | formulas flattened to one line (known) |
| other | 35 | 20 | mostly formulas and tables without the markers above; not all looked at |
| subscript with a letter | 8 | 6 | a subscript with a letter or a dot is lost: `kategorii T , T , T` instead of `T1b, T2b, T3b` (DU/2025/1490 art. 54) |
| heading in the text | 4 | 4 | a structure heading (`KSIĘGA PIERWSZA`, `TYTUŁ VI`) in the same paragraph as `(uchylony)` (KPC 2026/468 art. 1096) |
| article `…ł` | 4 | 4 | an article with the letter ł (art. 106ł) has no heading of its own and ends the previous one (DU/2025/633 art. 106l) |

## Limits

- Only articles present in both texts are checked; an error in an article added by a later amendment (1 182) or
  an error the PDF text layer shares is not seen.
- The class of an article "to review" is assigned by a pattern; only some of them were looked at.
- 2023 consolidated texts in the dataset: 2 of the compared statutes (gaps of the API in 2012–2024).
