== Cause of the bug (MP/2025/1128)
Not a shifted render. pdfminer puts a char's box from baseline+descent to baseline+descent+size, with the
descent from the font's /Descent. The placed fonts XRESBZ+Cambria / OCHBFR+Cambria (and every Cambria Math
subset) declare Ascent 3117, Descent -2464, FontBBox [-1475 -2464 2868 3117] (the math glyphs' extent). For
10.82 pt text the box therefore lies 15.8-26.7 pt below the baseline, i.e. on the next line. Page 21,
"elektromobilnos": pdfminer box top 600.32 / bottom 611.14; pdfium's tight box of "e" top 579.26 / bottom 584.55
(pdfium's loose box also ends at 611.14, same descent). The ink test sampled the next line's ink: "r" and "l"
fell where the next line had a gap ("elekt omobinos ci"); page 4 "Przegląd": glyphs at 113-121, boxes at 137-148.
Fix: _glyph_box() moves a box with more than 3/4 of it below the baseline (measured from the char matrix along
the glyph's up direction; upright and 90/180/270 degree text) to 0.3 below it; used for both tests in
_drop_hidden_placed. (Threshold 3/4, not 1/2: pdfminer adds a lowered subscript's text rise to the box but the
matrix does not show it.)
