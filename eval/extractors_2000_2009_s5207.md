# General PDF text extractors vs eli2md on Dz.U. 2000–2009 (sample s5207)

Script: [`extractors_2000_2009.py`](extractors_2000_2009.py). Per-act numbers: [`extractors_2000_2009_s5207.json`](extractors_2000_2009_s5207.json).

## Why these years

Dziennik Ustaw issues of 2000–2009 were typeset in QuarkXPress. Polish letters are set in fonts named `…PL`
(`Univers-PL`, `Univers-BoldPL`) with Mac Central European codes, but the PDF maps the codes as Mac Roman, so text
extraction gives `ROZPORZÑDZENIE`, `Si∏ Zbrojnych`, `u˝ytkowej`, `norm´`, `okreÊla` instead of `ROZPORZĄDZENIE`,
`Sił Zbrojnych`, `użytkowej`, `normę`, `określa`. The pages have two columns, and the PDF of one act is the pages of
the issue it is printed on, so its first and last page also carry the neighbouring acts.
The ELI API has no HTML for 16,759 of the 20,050 Dz.U. acts of 2000–2009 (checked 2026-10-01), so for those the PDF
is the only text.

## Method

- Sample: `sample_2000-2009_n60_s5207.json`, 60 random acts of 2000–2009 that have both PDF and HTML, drawn with seed
  5207 and committed before scoring (1ba1149). 6 acts that were in earlier 2000–2011 samples (eli2md was developed on
  some of them) are left out, and 1 act with a scanned page (2000/177), since none of the tools reads scans without OCR.
  53 acts remain: 34 statutes (ustawa), 13 Constitutional Tribunal rulings, 6 notices; 255,122 reference words.
  Acts with HTML are mostly statutes, the PDF-only ones mostly regulations; they are from the same issues and
  typesetting, but the numbers for the PDF-only acts may differ.
- Reference: the official HTML from the ELI API: main text, annexes given as text, footnotes (`evaluate.py`).
- Tools: pdfplumber 0.11.10 (`page.extract_text()`), pypdf 6.19.0 (`page.extract_text()`), PyMuPDF 1.28.2
  (`page.get_text()`), opendataloader-pdf 2.4.7 (markdown, default options, Java 21), each raw and `+table`;
  eli2md = this repository at 0.6.18 text (`convert()`, no OCR), main text + annexes + footnotes.
- `+table`: the extracted text with the Polish letters mapped back, for the whole text (no font information):
  ```python
  TABLE = str.maketrans({bytes([b]).decode("mac_roman"): bytes([b]).decode("mac_latin2")
                         for b in range(128, 256) if bytes([b]).decode("mac_latin2") in "ąćęłńśźżĄĆĘŁŃŚŹŻ"})
  text = text.translate(TABLE)  # only for Dz.U. 2000–2009 PDF text
  ```
  It also changes letters that really are `à ç è ê å Ñ Â Ê ¡ ¢ ´ ¸ ˚ ˝ ∏ ƒ` (e.g. French texts of treaties): in
  eli2md's text of all 10,845 acts of 2000–2005 such letters occur in 20 acts. eli2md maps only the chars of
  `…PL` fonts (`_mac_pl` in `eli2md/pdf.py`).
- Metrics on word tokens (lower case, punctuation ignored): **aligned** = words matched in order (difflib matching
  blocks; interleaved columns and blocks out of order lose words), **bag** = words matched ignoring order.
  Recall = matched / words of the HTML, precision = matched / words extracted. Footnotes are at the end in the HTML
  and inside the page text in a flat extraction: they are 8.9% of the reference words, so their place explains at
  most about 9 points of the difference between aligned and bag.

opendataloader-pdf converted all 53 PDFs (on 12 PDFs of 2026 it failed on 7 with `XRef loop`).

## What the differences are (checked on single acts)

- Letters: without the table every tool loses most words with a Polish letter (bag recall 0.68–0.79).
- pdfplumber reads the two columns line by line into one line (`Na podstawie art. 22 ust. 5 i 6 ustawy z dnia § 4. 1.
  Minimalna norma powierzchni u˝ytkowej`, DU/2005/668 p. 1) and glues one-letter words to the next word: `zdnia`,
  `wsprawie`, `oratyfikacji`, `z2001` (DU/2008/1010). The space after a one-letter word is narrower than pdfplumber's
  default word gap in these fonts.
- opendataloader-pdf has the words (bag R 0.992 with the table) but not their order: DU/2005/1495 starts with the
  running header, then the end of the previous act (poz. 1494), then pieces of the act's articles out of order
  (aligned R 0.156 for this act).
- Neighbouring acts: words out exceed the HTML by 9–26% for the flat tools. In a separate check of 30 random
  PDF-only acts of 2000–2005 (5 a year, not in this sample), the opendataloader-pdf text of 21 has the number heading
  of another position (the neighbouring act's start).
